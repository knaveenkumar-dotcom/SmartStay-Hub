About the Project

SMART STAY HUB

An IoT-based Smart PG Hostel Management System combining RFID attendance, PIR-based tailgating detection, environmental and safety monitoring, and remote appliance control — built on ESP32, MQTT (HiveMQ), and Node-RED.
